Why circular-obstacle pathfinding does not fit here

The article's method is a neat answer to a different question: the shortest route for a point through a fixed field of round obstacles. Our world has obstacles that are irregular polygons, intruders that move, aircraft that turn slowly, and a rule that the solution must be learned. Is any version of it still useful?

Possibly, but only on the training side. The environment itself knows the exact polygons, so it could compute the shortest-path distance to the goal around them and use that to shape the reward during training (a potential-based term, which does not change what the best policy is). Nothing from the planner would run when the policy is deployed, and the policy would still only see what the observation gives it. It addresses the static obstacles only. Today our weak spot is the intruders, so it is not the next thing to build.
